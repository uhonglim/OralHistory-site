About the project

Southern Voices is a collection of family oral histories. Each interviewer recorded one of their own relatives talking about moving, work, schooling and home in China and Macau.

What each interview includes

Recording
The full interview in the narrator’s own language.
Story
A short English account of the narrator’s life, written from the interview.
Quotes
Passages translated and edited for clarity, each linked to its moment in the recording.
Script
An edited reading version in English and Chinese, with a timestamp for every passage.

Contributors

The objects shown with each interview are representative illustrations chosen for its story, not the families’ own belongings.
