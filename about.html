About the Project

The project

Moving for the Next Generation collects interviews with students’ older relatives about work, school and the places they have lived.

The first interview is with Wang Rong Xuan’s grandfather. He and his wife ran a garment workshop in Fujian. He talks about moving the business to Anhai, buying a home in Xiamen for the children’s schooling, and travelling between the two places.

Background

The project began with a teacher’s brief on migration, education, language, work and family. Our first interview follows two decisions: where to run a business and where to send the children to school.

The interviews

Students record conversations in the language their relatives prefer. The original recordings are kept, and selected passages are prepared for the website. The narrator and interviewer review the text, translations and photographs before publication.

Translation

Wang’s interview mixes Hokkien and Mandarin. This website presents an English translation with audio excerpts in the original languages. The Chinese transcripts are kept with the project’s source files. Unclear words are marked in the English transcript.

Permission

Narrators choose which name, words, audio excerpts and photographs may appear online. They may skip questions, stop recording, correct the draft or ask the team to remove material. Permission to record does not include permission to publish. The team can remove material from this site, but cannot retrieve copies made by others.

Editing

Qwen3-ASR-1.7B and Breeze-ASR-26 produced the initial transcripts. AI also assisted with English translation, editing and website code. The current interview is a working draft awaiting a full check by a Hokkien speaker, family review and publication permission.

Contributors

  • Alan Lin: project lead, editing and website.
  • Wang Rong Xuan: first interviewer.
  • Wang Rong Xuan’s grandfather: first narrator.

The website’s collection format was informed by the Natick Veterans Oral History Project. The two projects are independent.
